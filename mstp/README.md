These files are **Christopher Carlson's** original Wolfram Language source and
example notebook, saved from his article:

  https://christophercarlson.com/portfolio/multi-scale-truchet-patterns/

They are kept here to read and to cross-check our own implementations
(`src/engine.js`, `dev/port.py`) against.  They are **not** covered by this
repository's MIT license — see `../LICENSE` and `../NOTICE`.  Don't copy them
into another project or a distribution build; ask Carlson about reuse terms.

Our engine was written from the published description of the geometry, not by
translating these files.
